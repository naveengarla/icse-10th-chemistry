## Source issues handled explicitly

- 2021–22 Q1(iii) gives 8 and 13 as electronic configurations; these are atomic numbers.
- 2021–22 Q1(v) calls O₂ a compound; it is a covalent molecule of an element.
- 2019 Q5 retains the instruction not to repeat an element. The cathode answer is A (H⁺), because B was already used in part (i).
- 2025 Q5 diagram places G in group 15: valency 3, not the handwritten 0.
- 2025 Q4 uses an inappropriate X⁴⁺ hint for covalent XY₂.
- Unit test Q3: Na is larger than Li.
- Additional Q7(a)-15 is not unique; sodium is the book’s reference example.
- Noble-gas radius comparisons are given with the book convention and a scientific clarification.
- Electron affinity comparisons refer to energy released. Cl exceeds F in the source values.
- p.9 reducing-agent footnote and p.4 seventh-period count are corrected.
